Gyan AI integration for API-created farms is planned as a future paid add-on.

Invoices are emailed with a Razorpay payment link and are due within 15 days of issue. If not paid within this window, API access only is automatically suspended until the outstanding invoice is settled — your dashboard and all farm data, including farms created via the API, remain fully visible and accessible in the dashboard throughout.

A Business Account's own farms created directly through the dashboard (rather than via the API) are billed at the standard individual farm-plan rates in Section 4 above, and receive the full individual feature set. Such farms are visible only on the dashboard and are never exposed through the API.
