Prewarm a region Vyom Engine — admin only

Deliberate, targeted use only — for a known upcoming rollout (e.g. a partner campaign starting in a specific district next month). This is not a tool for covering all of India — see the item-8 research notes on why blind national pre-processing wastes compute/storage and doesn't beat CDSE's own rate limits anyway. Every fetch this dispatches runs in the background (non-priority) and can never block a real farmer's own onboarding request.

Region boundary

Paste a GeoJSON Polygon or MultiPolygon for the target area — e.g. a district boundary drawn and exported from geojson.io.

Coordinates are [longitude, latitude], matching standard GeoJSON order.
~4.4km at the equator by default. Smaller = denser/more overlapping coverage, more cells dispatched.
